Why these companies

A career is a sequence of bets on where infrastructure was going. These were the bets.

  • RightScale (2013): early to cloud when "cloud" still meant one vendor. RightScale's thesis was that orchestrating resources through APIs would matter more than owning them: multi-cloud management, ServerTemplates, automation across AWS, Azure, SoftLayer, Rackspace and Google Compute Engine years before "multicloud" was a category. Its co-founder and CTO, Thorsten von Eicken, had been a Cornell computer-science professor who co-authored the U-Net work with Werner Vogels, later Amazon's CTO. Working the AWS alliance from inside that company was a front-row seat to the API-first, orchestrate-don't-own model everything since has been built on.
  • Smartsheet (2014): SaaS at enterprise scale before it was assumed: rollouts from 2,000 to 200,000 seats, API partners as the growth channel, SSO and governance as the buying criteria. The playbook for how software actually lands in a large company.
  • Internap / INAP (2015 – 2018): a monster in networking: a global backbone with route-optimized transit and high-density data centers, plus OpenStack, bare-metal cloud and colocation on top. It is where Layer 2–7, peering, latency and the physical substrate under "the cloud" became first-hand knowledge, the part most cloud architects never touch.
  • Rubrik (2018 – 2019): the data layer reinvented as a cloud-native, API-first platform: backup, recovery, replication and search as one distributed system with an open API, at the moment ransomware made data management a board-level problem. Field CTO for the channel there meant teaching the ecosystem how orchestration and Open APIs change operations.
  • Nutanix (2019 –): the hybrid multicloud bet made real: the same platform on-prem and in AWS, Azure and Google Cloud, with NC2 taken from a fifteen-person incubation team to a 250-person organization. The v4 APIs, Flow Virtual Networking and infrastructure-as-code on top of it are the current chapter; AI infrastructure, GPUs, storage and the cost model next to the data, is the next one.
