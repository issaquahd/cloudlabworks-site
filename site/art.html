Art

Two shelves. The first is work made here: Raku-fired ceramics, blown glass, thrown pots, acrylic and watercolor, and the metal and driftwood that get mixed in. The second is what feeds it: artists, single pieces, photographs and writing that Alex keeps coming back to. Earth tones first, then the loud colour set against them.

Made here

  • Raku ceramicsFirst pieces arrivingPhotographed from the studio, titled and dated as they go up. Crackle glazes, the copper flash, the smoke where the reduction took.
  • GlassBlown and fusedVessels and flat work; the colour goes in hot and the light finishes it.
  • PotteryThrown stonewareCups and bowls that get used. Natural clay bodies, a few loud glazes.
  • PaintAcrylic and watercolorPacific Northwest subjects, mostly: water, weather, the driftwood coast.
