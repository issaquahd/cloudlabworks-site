ASR: the Architecture State Register

Every architecture-significant change to the lab and the estate, logged the turn it ships: what changed, why, what a failure there could reach, and how it is undone. Published because a lab that says "zero trust" should be able to show its own change log. Identifiers are masked: no hostnames, addresses, account or ticket numbers, no people beyond the operator and the agent, and nothing from an employer.

The rule.
  1. A change that adds a service, a boundary, a credential path or a data store gets a row before it is called live.
  2. Every row names its reach: what the next failure touches if this piece is compromised. If the reach cannot be named, the change waits.
  3. Every row names its undo. Reversibility is engineered, not requested: branch, snapshot, backup, or a one-line rollback.
  4. Rows are never deleted. A reversal is a new row and the old one is marked superseded.
  5. Approval is required only where a change is both irreversible and external. Everything else proceeds and is logged.

The register also plays. Code as Music carries ASR as Music: the register, one drop: one bar per row, reggae one drop at 74 BPM, the bass falling as a row's reach widens, the skank ghosted where nothing has shipped yet, a minor turn where a row is held. Regenerated from this table whenever it changes.
