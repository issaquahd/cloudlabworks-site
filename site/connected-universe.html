The check

The Schwarzschild-proton claim is the one with a number attached, which means it's the one that can be checked without appeal to anyone's authority — including this site's. A Schwarzschild radius is the radius at which an object's escape velocity equals the speed of light — the definition of a black hole's event horizon: r_s = 2GM / c². Two independently measured, uncontested numbers go in: the proton's mass and its charge radius.

G = 6.67430e-11        # m^3 kg^-1 s^-2, CODATA
c = 299792458.0        # m/s, exact by SI definition
m_p = 1.67262192369e-27  # kg, CODATA proton mass
r_p = 0.8409e-15       # m, proton charge radius
                       # (muonic hydrogen spectroscopy, Pohl et al. 2010, Nature)

That is not a rounding error in anyone's model; it is the actual physical claim, checked against the actual measured constants.
