On the water, Washington rules for Southern Residents (endangered): stay at least 1,000 yards away; under 7 knots within half a mile; engines off if they surface within 400 yards. Bigg's orcas: 200 yards. When you can't tell which you're looking at, treat them as Southern Residents. Be Whale Wise.

The pattern behind this page was drawn for the site from Coast Salish design vocabulary (the ovoid, the crescent, the trigon, the formline) with respect for the Coast Salish peoples whose waters these are. It reproduces no artist's work; the lab sits on Snoqualmie land in Duvall.
