Cloud and AI infrastructure, designed to be run.

Cloud Lab Works is a working lab and a place to collaborate live on projects: hybrid multicloud, AI infrastructure from the rack to the region, agentic systems that run real infrastructure, and technical writing that shows the work. Current interests: osmotic communication between agents, multi-agent swarms, advanced harnesses, next-generation routing (graph-based), and self-learning systems.

Written for the engineer who has to build it, not the slide that has to sell it. Whole system first, then the parts; every design grounded in what it costs to operate.

  • Projects, in the openThe lab builds things and publishes them: agentic operations on real infrastructure, Infrastructure as Music, NC2 on AWS as code. Public repos under issaquahd and cloudlabworks; bring a problem and build it here with us.

Not a consultancy. Nothing here is offered for hire; it is a lab, open to collaboration. Bring a project.

Orca Watch

The Salish Sea is the lab's back yard and the orca is on the header. Orca Watch gathers every live feed we know of: sighting maps, cameras, hydrophones. Each opening at its source.

  • Where they are nowOrcaWatch's live sighting map for Puget Sound and the San Juans, and Orca Network's vetted Sighting Network.

Alex's story

Alex Alvord grew up in the Pacific Northwest, navigating it with his hands and feet. With a long career in cloud and IT behind him, he dives head first into the magic AI can provide with the right harness and tooling: osmotic communication, multi-agent swarms, advanced harnesses, next-gen routing built on graphs, and systems that learn from their own runs.

His background runs through Raku-fired ceramics, glass art, pottery, and painting; he mixes mediums with the Pacific Northwest's great driftwood coast. The work, and what feeds it, is on the Art page.

By trade: an Advisory Solutions Architect, Hybrid Multicloud, whose career has run through cloud, data center, and infrastructure software. Certifications include AWS Solutions Architect – Professional, Microsoft Azure Solutions Architect Expert, Google Professional Cloud Architect, and Nutanix NCP-MCI for AWS and for Azure, the NC2 exams he helped write before sitting them himself. He founded the Seattle Nutanix User Group and works from a home lab in Duvall that doubles as the test bench for everything published here. Projects, career, recognition, and code →
