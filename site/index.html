I build cloud and AI infrastructure, then I run it.

Hi, I'm Alex. Cloud Lab Works is my lab in Duvall, Washington, and the place I build things out loud: hybrid multicloud, AI infrastructure from the rack to the region, and agents that run real infrastructure (mine). If you want to build something together, this is the door. Right now I'm deep in how agents talk to each other, multi-agent swarms, harnesses, graph-based routing, and systems that learn from their own runs.

I write for the person who has to build it and keep it running, not for the slide. I start with the whole system, then the parts, and I always ask what it costs to operate. Twenty years of doing this taught me the second question matters more than the first.

Orca Watch

The Salish Sea is my back yard and the orca is on the header for a reason. Orca Watch is every live feed I know of in one place: sighting maps, cameras, hydrophones. Each one opens at its source, so the people who run them get the visit.

  • Where they are nowOrcaWatch's live sighting map for Puget Sound and the San Juans, and Orca Network's vetted Sighting Network.
  • Live camerasOrcaLab's cams on Hanson Island, Cracroft Point and the Robson Bight rubbing beaches, and The Whale Museum's Lime Kiln lighthouse cam on San Juan Island.
  • Live hydrophonesOrcasound. Put on headphones and listen to Haro Strait and Puget Sound for the Southern Residents.

About me

I grew up in the Pacific Northwest and I still navigate it with my hands and feet: bikes, rivers, the playground with my kids. After a long run in cloud and IT, I dove head first into what AI can do with the right harness and tooling. Agents that talk to each other, multi-agent swarms, graph-based routing, systems that learn from their own runs. It is the most fun I have had in this industry, and I have been in it a while.

Off the keyboard I make things with my hands: Raku-fired ceramics, blown glass, pottery, painting. I like mixing mediums, and the driftwood coast out here does most of the work.

By trade I'm an Advisory Solutions Architect for hybrid multicloud. My career has run through cloud, data center, and infrastructure software, with AWS Solutions Architect Professional, Azure Solutions Architect Expert, and Google Professional Cloud Architect along the way.
