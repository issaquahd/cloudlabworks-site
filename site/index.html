Alex's story

Alex Alvord grew up in the Pacific Northwest, navigating it with his hands and feet. With a long career in cloud and IT behind him, he dives head first into the magic AI can provide with the right harness and tooling — osmotic communication, multi-agent swarms, advanced harnesses, next-gen routing built on graphs, and systems that learn from their own runs.

His background runs through Raku-fired ceramics, glass art, pottery, and painting — mixing mediums with the Pacific Northwest's great driftwood coast.

By trade: an Advisory Solutions Architect, Hybrid Multicloud, whose career has run through cloud, data center, and infrastructure software. Certifications include AWS Solutions Architect – Professional, Microsoft Azure Solutions Architect Expert, and Google Professional Cloud Architect.
