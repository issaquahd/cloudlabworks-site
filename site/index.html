Mentoring

It started the other way round. An undergraduate business professor saw the drive in a student who was running a store and wiring up a point-of-sale system, and took him under his wing. Alex has been paying that back since 2016, and the rule he works by is his own: coaching and mentoring are very different things — who do you really want to serve?

  • Pacific Lutheran University — Econ Mentoring ProgramAlumni mentor in the Department of Economics' Alumni & Student Connections program since February 2016: career-field insight and industry connections for students heading into their first roles. Ten years and counting.
  • National Society of Sales EngineersMentor to aspiring sales engineers across verticals since 2022 — two full candidate cycles of about twelve weeks each — and a judge at the 2023 National Sales Engineering Conference.
  • PreSales CollectiveTechnical member and mentor in the community for pre-sales professionals, 2022 – 2025: the craft of the demo, the PoC, and the architecture conversation that has to survive contact with procurement.
  • Technology Access FoundationSTEM volunteer since 2021, teaching 8th-grade classes from real incident work: "Using Analytics to Fight Ransomware" for the core-classes group, "Hybrid Multi-Cloud PaaS" for the robotics elective.
  • Inside the jobAt Nutanix, global enablement for the NC2 unit as it grew from roughly fifteen people to more than 250 — onboarding architects, reviewing designs, and turning field escalations into best-practice guides and validated designs the next engineer can use. Founder of the Seattle Nutanix User Group (2020) and its Anchorage sibling, folded into Seattle in 2022.

Also: Watermark's Supporting Women in Technology community, the WTIA, and the FinOps and DevOps communities. Full list on LinkedIn. Looking for a mentor for a student cohort, a new SE, or an engineer moving into cloud architecture? Get in touch.
