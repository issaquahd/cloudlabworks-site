IaC Live Music!

The lab, as sound. Every deploy, every health check, every fault in the Duvall lab drives an instrument — Sonic Pi on the machine that runs the agents. This page plays the same music from the same state, in your browser, and keeps the lab's own recordings by date.

lab · loading
Press play. Your browser becomes the instrument; nothing streams from the lab.

How it works: the lab publishes its state — every site answering, or not, and the last few events — as a small file. The page reads it every half minute and plays it: E major, a slow plucked arpeggio and an airy pad while everything answers; minor, slower, with a low tone underneath, while something is down; a rising bell figure when a deploy lands. The score is code in git; the browser is a second copy of the instrument.

Recorded from the lab's own instrument: twenty seconds around each fault, recovery and deploy, assembled nightly into one take per day.
