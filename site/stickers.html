Stickers

Twenty-six laptop stickers, photographed and cut out by the lab's own pipeline (Apple Vision for the outline, a colour mask where Vision gave up, a convex hull of the printed colour for the one shot through a plastic sleeve, a taper correction for the ones shot at an angle, grey-world white balance for the one the camera turned blue). The old ones are from the early cloud years; the new ones are from the bench this month. Pinned to a cork board because that is where stickers go when they are not on a laptop.

Marks belong to their owners; these are photographs of Alex's own stickers. The cut-outs also appear beside the work they came from on Work and Certifications.
