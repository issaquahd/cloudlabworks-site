 Six instance types, two storage architectures
 =============================================

 Not a VM. Not nested virtualization.
 Not Google Bare Metal Solution (explicitly unsupported).

 LOCAL SSD: AOS on the node's own Titanium NVMe
   instance                        vCPU   memory     NVMe
   -----------------------------   ----   --------   ----------
   z3-highmem-192-highlssd-metal    192   1,536 GB   12 x 6 TB
   c4-standard-288-lssd-metal       288   1,080 GB    6 x 3 TB
   c4-highmem-288-lssd-metal        288   2,232 GB    6 x 3 TB

 HYPERDISK BALANCED: no local storage, remote NVMe
   c3-standard-192-metal            192     768 GB   15-120 TB
   c3-highcpu-192-metal             192     512 GB   15-120 TB
   c3-highmem-192-metal             192   1,536 GB   15-120 TB

 C3 rules: no resize from the GCP console, no adding disks
 to a node, one volume fails and the whole node is replaced.

 23 regions, but availability is PER INSTANCE TYPE.
 Only us-central1, europe-west4 and asia-southeast1 have all six.
