 Flow Virtual Networking is mandatory here
 =========================================

 AWS and Azure:   Flow is a choice.
 Google Cloud:    Flow is the only option. No VLAN networking.

 And lighter than Azure: NO Flow Gateway VMs required.
 That removes a VNet, two VMs and the BGP plumbing.

   user VM --- Flow overlay subnet --- Flow VPC
                     |  Geneve between hosts
                     |  Prism Central is the control plane
                     v
              NAT range / floating IPs --- Google Cloud,
                                           BigQuery, GKE, internet
                                           or PGA / PSA / PSC

 Do not create Google Cloud entities in the Flow external
 NAT subnet. The network your VMs see is not the GCP network.
